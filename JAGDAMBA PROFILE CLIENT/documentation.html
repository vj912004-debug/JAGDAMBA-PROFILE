1. System Vision & Goals

The BroBond ERP is a mission-critical platform designed to digitize and automate field sales operations. It provides real-time visibility into employee movement, order workflows, and inventory status, ensuring operational integrity through AI-driven verification.

Operational Goal

Eliminate manual paperwork and track 100% of field activities via GPS and automated logs.

Security Goal

Prevent proxy attendance using Face Recognition and GPS Geofencing verification.

Growth Goal

Optimize sales routes (Beat Planning) to increase retailer touchpoints by 30%.
