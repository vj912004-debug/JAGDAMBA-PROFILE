The Technical Encyclopedia

A comprehensive technical specification for the Shree Nakoda Beverages ERP ecosystem, detailing field sales automation, AI verification, and live tracking logic.

1. System Vision

The BroBond ERP is a mission-critical platform designed to bridge the gap between management and field operations. It transforms traditional beverage distribution into a data-driven ecosystem through real-time telemetry, facial authentication, and automated audit workflows.

Field Automation

Eliminating manual paperwork with real-time order entry and GPS-validated visits.

Verification AI

Ensuring integrity through Face Recognition and GPS-tagged image verification.

Live Intelligence

Providing management with a bird's-eye view of all field movements on a live map.

B. Field Beat & Route Planning

Algorithmic route optimization for sales executives.

  • Route Assignment: Admins define daily "Beats" for specific retailers.
  • Visit Sequence: Optimized order of retailers based on GPS distance.
  • Compliance: System blocks order entry if executive is >50m from retailer GPS.
